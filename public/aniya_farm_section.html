📍 The Aniya Farm

Aniya’s pilot operations are anchored in the mountain farms of Tarlac, Philippines—between 800 to 1000 meters above sea level—where we grow high-elevation coffee alongside other potential intercrops.

Each hectare supports up to 800 coffee trees and sustains four rural families through meaningful employment. The land is managed in partnership with Farm To Cup Philippines and Coffea ReAL, a farming collective rooted in transparency, spiritual stewardship, and land care.

Farming practices include:

  • Agroforestry: planting coffee under shade trees and fruit-bearing crops
  • Permaculture: designing self-sustaining, regenerative farm systems
  • Soil regeneration: composting, minimal tillage, and cover cropping
  • Intercropping (Legacy users only): vegetable, fruits and livestock integrations
