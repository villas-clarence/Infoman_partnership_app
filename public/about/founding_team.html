Our Founders

Meet the passionate team driving Aniya's mission forward

Aidrian Fatallar

Founder & CEO of Aniya Philippines

At just 19 years old, Aidrian launched his first coffee venture, The Four-Leaf Clover Coffee, driven by his deep admiration for local Filipino coffee and the communities that grow it.

That early experience opened his eyes to a systemic issue: a broken link between everyday consumers and the farmers who make their morning brews possible. Aidrian's passion has always extended beyond profit — he believes in purpose-driven entrepreneurship and in building platforms that uplift others.

With Aniya, he's reimagining how people engage with agriculture, bringing tech, storytelling, and shared impact together. For Aidrian, this isn't just a business — it's a movement to redefine farming for the next generation.

Mark Andrie Codog

Co-founder & Tech Lead, Aniya Philippines

Mark is not only one of the original baristas at The Four-Leaf Clover Coffee but also a passionate IT student who sees technology as a tool for positive change.

His dual love for coffee and systems design made him the perfect co-founder to lead Aniya's digital platform. From building backend frameworks to ensuring the user experience feels simple and human, Mark's technical vision ensures Aniya's mission is not only inspiring but scalable.

His work bridges tradition with innovation — enabling people to access real farms with just a few clicks.

Eli Natividad

Co-founder & Farm Director, Aniya Philippines

Eli brings over a decade of hands-on experience in farming, agroforestry, and sustainable land development. As the founder of Farm To Cup Philippines, his leadership has helped smallholder farmers increase productivity while staying rooted in environmental responsibility.

Eli is the heart of Aniya's farming operations. He ensures that every tree planted is not just grown — but nurtured in alignment with regenerative principles.

For Eli, Aniya is a tool that connects the future of farming with the wisdom of the land.

Together, we're cultivating a sustainable future for Filipino agriculture
